Figure Shelf Support

Figure Shelf lets children browse animals, creatures, robots, animatronics and dinosaurs, change their colors and accessories, make them move, and see them in the room with the camera. Everything is open from the start.

How to use it

  • Pick a figure: tap one in the list at the bottom.
  • Turn and zoom: drag sideways to turn it, pinch to zoom, double-tap to go back.
  • Full screen: swipe the menu down once to keep only the actions, and again to hide them. Tap "Show menu" to bring it back.
  • See in my room: move the phone slowly until the floor is found. Tap the floor to move the figure, pinch to resize, twist to turn.
  • Undo and Reset: the round arrow undoes the last change. Reset goes back to the original look.

Common questions

The room view is black. Allow camera access in Settings > Figure Shelf > Camera.

There's no "See in my room" button. The device doesn't support augmented reality. Everything else works.

How do I turn sound off? Tap the speaker button at the top.

Where is the parents' info? Press and hold the small "i" button for 3 seconds.
