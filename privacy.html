Figure Shelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Figure Shelf is a calm 3D toy app, suitable for all ages. We built it so that it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Figure Shelf has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not send any information over the internet.

Camera

When you open "See in my room", the app uses the camera to place a figure on your floor with augmented reality. The camera image is used live on your device only. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Data stored on the device

The app remembers a few choices on your device, such as the last figure, its colors and accessories, and whether sound is on. This stays on the device and is removed when you delete the app.

Young users

Figure Shelf is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is nothing to share, sell or delete. Links to this page inside the app sit behind a parents-only press-and-hold button.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
