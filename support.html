How to use it

  1. Pick a figure from the list at the bottom. Drag sideways on it to turn it around.
  2. Zoom and look closer: pinch to zoom, move two fingers to slide, double-tap to go back.
  3. Go full screen: swipe the menu down once to keep only the moves, and again to hide them. Tap "Show menu" to bring it back.
  4. See it in your room: tap "See in my room" and move the phone slowly until the floor is found. Tap the floor to move the figure, pinch to resize, twist to turn.

Common questions

The room view is black

Allow camera access in Settings > Figure Shelf > Camera, then open the room view again.

There's no "See in my room" button

Your device doesn't support augmented reality. Everything else in the app works normally.

How do I turn sound off?

Tap the speaker button at the top of the screen.

Where is the parents' info?

Press and hold the small "i" button for 3 seconds. It shows the version, the privacy policy and this page.

Does the app collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics, and the camera image never leaves your device. See the Privacy Policy.
